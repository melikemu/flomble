Privacy Policy

Flomble  ·  Last updated: June 24, 2026  ·  Melike Mühür

Flomble is a color memory game. This policy describes what information the app collects, why, and how it is used.

Information Stored on Your Device

Flomble saves the following data locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is not shared with any third party and is used solely to provide the app's functionality.

  • Game progress — completed levels, star ratings, and lifetime match count.
  • Daily resources — lives, joker uses, and daily reset timestamps. These reset each day and are never sent off-device.
  • Streak and challenge history — your current streak and daily challenge results, stored locally.
  • League data — your weekly points and league tier. The leaderboard you see is populated with computer-generated players; no real player data is collected or shared at this stage.
  • Display name — an optional name you choose for the in-app leaderboard. This name is stored locally on your device. It is not linked to your identity and is never transmitted to external servers.
  • Settings — your preferences such as language, dark mode, card design, text size, and sound options.

Advertising

Flomble uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party service operated by Google LLC. When ads are shown, AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location (country/region level)
  • Device type, operating system, and language
  • Ad interaction data (views and taps)

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Users who purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase will not be shown any advertisements.

In-App Purchases

Flomble offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase through the Google Play Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google. Flomble does not collect or store any payment information.

Optional Research Participation

Flomble includes an optional feature that contributes anonymous gameplay data to color perception research. This feature is off by default and only activates if you explicitly choose to participate when prompted in the app.

If you opt in, the following anonymous data may be sent to our secure servers:

  • Which color pairs were shown and whether they were matched correctly
  • Response time for each card selection (in milliseconds)
  • A randomly generated anonymous identifier (no name, email, or account)

This data contains no personal information and cannot be used to identify you. It is used solely to understand color perception patterns. You can withdraw your participation at any time through the app's Settings screen.

Internet Connection

Flomble connects to the internet for two purposes: to display advertisements (AdMob) and, if you have opted in, to submit anonymous research data. The core gameplay — levels, progress, and daily challenges — works entirely offline.

Children's Privacy

Flomble is rated for users aged 4 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The optional research participation feature is designed to collect only anonymous, non-personal gameplay events. If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

All data stored locally on your device can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data through your device settings. If you participated in the optional research feature, anonymous event data stored on our servers does not contain any identifier that could be used to locate or delete your specific records.

Your Rights (EEA and UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Since Flomble collects no personal data without your explicit consent, there is generally no personal data for us to provide or delete. For any questions, please contact us at the address below.
